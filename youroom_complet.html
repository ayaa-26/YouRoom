Learn

Short reads for a clearer head

Mind
Understanding stress

Stress is the body's response to demands. Short bursts can help you focus, but constant stress affects sleep, mood and concentration. Helpful habits include regular breaks, movement, time outdoors and talking to someone you trust.

Mind
Understanding anxiety

Anxiety is a normal alarm system that sometimes fires too often. Slow breathing, naming the worry, and facing small fears step by step can reduce its grip. If worry affects daily life for weeks, speak with a professional.

Body
Sleep and mental health

Sleep and mood influence each other. Keep a steady wake up time, dim screens before bed, limit caffeine after noon and keep your bedroom cool and dark.

Body
Moving your body

You do not need a gym. A 10 minute walk, some stretching or dancing in your kitchen can lift energy and ease tension. Pick something you enjoy and keep it small enough to repeat.

Mind
Burnout: early signs

Common signs are constant exhaustion, cynicism about work, reduced performance and feeling detached. Rest alone is rarely enough; look at workload, boundaries and support.

Mind
Building self esteem

Notice your inner critic and answer it the way you would answer a friend. Keep a short list of things you handled well each week and set goals small enough to finish.

Mind
Dealing with negative thoughts

Thoughts are not facts. Try to spot patterns such as all or nothing thinking or catastrophizing, then test them: what is the evidence for and against this thought? The thought record in the Reflect room walks you through it.

People
How to support a friend

Listen without rushing to fix. Say what you have noticed, ask how they are really doing, and encourage professional help if things seem heavy. Check in again later.

People
Is therapy for me?

Therapy is for anyone who wants support, not only for crises. If distress lasts, affects your work, relationships or sleep, or you simply want to understand yourself better, it is worth exploring.

Seven questions about the last two weeks. The GAD-7 is a widely used screening questionnaire.

About YouRoom

YouRoom is a small, free project that makes mental health education and simple coping tools easy to reach. It does not collect your data: check ins, thought records, journal entries and screening results are stored only in your browser. Clearing your browser data erases them.
